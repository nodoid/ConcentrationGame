DESCRIPTION  (1045/10000 characters)
------------------------------------
The classic memory game, played in a fog-bound forest with a deck of dark tarot cards.

Sixteen cards lie face down: eight pairs. Turn two over. If they match, they stay face up; if they don't, they turn back and that's one wrong guess. Find all eight pairs before you run out of guesses.

HOW HARD DO YOU WANT IT?

• Easy: 20 wrong guesses allowed
• Medium: 10 wrong guesses allowed
• Hard: just 5 - you will need a good memory and a little luck

Every pair you find scores points, and harder games score more. Your ten best scores are kept in the high score table, with the difficulty each was set on.

FEATURES

• Eight gothic tarot cards: the Devil, the Lovers, the Magician, the Knight and more
• Three difficulty levels
• Ten-place high score table, saved on your PC
• Atmospheric sound: screams, slicing cards and howling wolves
• Quick games - a round takes a minute or two

Based on the original Concentration by Shea Ehrenberger; .NET MAUI version by Paul F. Johnson. No ads, no tracking, no accounts and no internet connection needed.

NOTES FOR CERTIFICATION  (689/2000 characters)
----------------------------------------------
Offline, single-player memory game; no account, sign-in, network connection or purchase is needed. On the start screen click Easy, Medium or Hard. Sixteen cards are shown face down: click one to turn it, then click a second. A matching pair stays face up; a pair that doesn't match turns back after a second and counts as a wrong guess (shown under the board). The game ends when all eight pairs are found or the wrong guesses reach the level's limit (Easy 20, Medium 10, Hard 5); Reset Game and High Scores then appear. High scores are saved in the app's own data folder. The package is a .NET MAUI (WinUI 3) desktop app; runFullTrust is its only capability (see restricted capabilities).

RESTRICTED CAPABILITIES  (502/1000 characters)
----------------------------------------------
runFullTrust: Concentration is a packaged desktop app built with .NET 10 MAUI on WinUI 3 and the Windows App SDK. Every packaged desktop (Win32) app needs runFullTrust to start its executable (Windows.FullTrustApplication entry point); it is used only to run the game's own process. The game does not use the internet, other apps, the user's documents or system settings, and needs no elevation. It reads the mouse, touch and pen, plays sound, and saves its high score table in its own app data folder.
